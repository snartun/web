# Global demand for casual mobile genres in 2024–2026, with a focus on genres that work both solo and as 1v1 duels

Notes are in English. Every claim has a source. Notes marked "Inference" are mine. Download and revenue figures come from market-intelligence estimators (AppMagic, Sensor Tower), so they are estimates, not company-reported numbers. Different estimators often disagree.

## 1. Which genres lead downloads and revenue in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
Puzzle is the largest casual genre by revenue, and it is still growing: about $14.4B IAP on mobile in 2025 per Sensor Tower, up from $12.2B in 2024. Downloads are falling across the market. The exceptions are hyper-casual and hybrid-casual, where downloads and IAP are still growing. Block puzzle, sort, screw and merge are the fastest-growing subgenres. The download charts are led by ad-driven, simple-input games (Block Blast, Subway Surfers, Hole.io, Paper.io 2) and by casual multiplayer staples (Ludo King, 8 Ball Pool).

### Cited Findings
**Market totals**
- In 2025, global mobile game IAP revenue was $81.75B (+1.3% YoY). Downloads fell 7.2% to 50.41B, the second straight annual decline. Time spent rose 0.9%. — [Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026 (summary by GameDevReports)](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- In 2024, mobile game IAP was about $82B (+4% YoY). — [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-mobile-gaming-rebounds-in-2024-as-player-engagement-and-spending-reach-new-highs). **Ads**

### Inferences
- The download leaders are dominated by games with one-touch or very simple input and very short sessions: block puzzle, io games (Hole.io, Paper.io 2, Snake Clash), runners, mahjong/tile games, Ludo and pool. These are mostly ad-monetized and fit browser play well. Revenue, by contrast, is concentrated in deep live-ops match-3 and merge games, which a small team cannot easily replicate.
- Block puzzle, sort and screw are growing fast in IAP from a small base, which suggests the hybrid-casual "simple puzzle + light meta" format is still under-saturated. The 10x and 2x figures are from AppMagic.

### Gaps

## 2. Which casual titles have strong 1v1 or versus play, and why does 1v1 work?

### Takeaway
Several of the most downloaded casual games are 1v1 or small-PvP at their core:
- Ludo King: 151M downloads in 2025, more than 1.25B lifetime.
- 8 Ball Pool: 113M downloads in 2025, real-time 1v1.
- Chess.com: more than 6B games in 2025, about 8.7–10.2M DAU.
- io games: Hole.io, Paper.io 2 and Snake Clash, all at 100M+.
- Clash Royale: $453M in 2025.

Match Masters showed that PvP match-3 can monetize ($200M+ lifetime by 2023). Squad Busters, a casual Supercell PvP title, was shut down, so PvP alone does not guarantee success. Published evidence on *why* 1v1 works is mostly qualitative or comes from vendors.

### Cited Findings
**Ludo King**
- 151M downloads in 2025, #6 worldwide. — [Mobilegamer.biz/AppMagic](https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-mobile-game-downloads-of-2025/)
- More than 1.25B lifetime downloads by mid-2025, and India's most downloaded game since 2017. — [Sensor Tower India insights 2025](https://sensortower.com/blog/india-mobile-game-insights-2025)
- MAU estimates conflict: AppGoblin's modeled figure is about 35–55M. — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.ludo.king)

**8 Ball Pool (Miniclip)**
- 113M downloads in 2025, #11 worldwide. — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-mobile-game-downloads-of-2025/)
- US weekly revenue peaked at about $426K in late July 2025 (Sensor Tower Q3 2025 US). — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-unified-top-5-2d%20realistic%20games-units-us-60222863241bc16eb83eea24)
- Miniclip reported 1.6B matches per month in 2021 (old data). — [European Gaming 2021](https://europeangaming.eu/portal/latest-news/2021/06/02/93555/miniclips-8-ball-pool-marks-decade-since-launch-as-monthly-match-count-hits-1-6-billion/)
- Lifetime revenue was more than $417M as of 2018, with the US at 52% (old). — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/8-ball-pool-revenue)
- Third-party MAU and revenue estimates conflict widely (AppGoblin: about $1M+/month, IAP/ad split inconsistent). — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.miniclip.eightballpool)

**Chess.com**
- More than 6B games played in 2025. — [Chess.com 2025 Year in Review](https://www.chess.com/article/view/year-in-review-2025)
- Average DAU was 9.7M in Q1 2026 (+17.7% YoY) and 10.2M in Q2 2026 (+25.7% YoY). — [Chess.com board report Q1 2026](https://www.chess.com/board-reports/2026-q1); [Q2 2026](https://www.chess.com/board-reports/2026-q2)
- 15–20M+ games on a typical day. — [Chess.com stats](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-stats)

**Match Masters (Candivore)**

- About $211M lifetime IAP on 42M downloads as of Q1 2023, and $28.4M in Q1 2023. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/?p=19676)
- More than $200M and 50M installs. — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/candivores-match-masters-passes-200m-50m-installs-as-it-sets-sights-on-us-market/)
- 90% of revenue from IAP. — [Unity case study](https://unity.com/resources/candivore)

**io and "versus-lite" games**
- 2025 downloads: Hole.io 121M, Paper.io 2 102M, Snake Clash 104M. — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-mobile-game-downloads-of-2025/)
- Inference: these games are mostly arena or "vs bots" formats rather than strict 1v1.

**Clash Royale**
- $453M IAP in 2025, a real-time 1v1 duel of about 3 minutes. — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/the-top-grossing-mobile-games-of-2025/)
- Brawl Stars fell to $271M (2024: $626M). — same source

**Squad Busters (Supercell)**
- Supercell ended development in Oct 2025: IAP was disabled on 30 Oct 2025, a final update came in Dec 2025, and the servers close in 2026. It is Supercell's first live game to be shut down. — [Supercell](https://supercell.com/en/news/squad-development-ending/); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/supercell-will-shut-down-squad-busters-next-year)
- About $70M revenue in its first year (AppMagic). — [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-squad-busters-roblox-fortnite-color-block-jam-superplay-metacore-more/)

**Stumble Guys**
- Declining in 2025. In the US (Q3 2025), weekly downloads fell to about 37K, weekly revenue to about $24K, and active users to about 2.9M (Sensor Tower). — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q3-unified-top-5-platformer%20games-revenue-us-600abc40241bc16eb850d726)

**NYT Games**
- The NYT launched its first 2-player game in Jan 2026 (reported by AP, carried by WTOP/AOL). — [AOL/AP](https://www.aol.com/articles/just-york-times-first-2-140209355.html)
- Creative Review names it "Crossplay". — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/new-york-times-games-wordle-crossplay-design-jonathan-knight/)
- I could not fetch the full article, so I could not verify the format details (async vs real-time).

**Why 1v1 and PvP work (qualitative)**
- Keep PvP in casual games "casual and simplified": quick to join and finish, low concentration. Examples:
  - Disney Emoji Blitz: about 1-minute real-time match-3 face-offs.
  - Board Kings: async board raids.
  - Hogwarts Mystery: async duels that reuse the single-player battle system.
  - Love Nikki: async league ladder.
  — [GameRefinery, May 2019 (old)](https://www.gamerefinery.com/4-great-examples-pvp-modes-casual-games/)
- Async is the dominant mobile multiplayer approach (Words With Friends is the canonical example). Real-time play has struggled on mobile because of matchmaking, session design and monetization constraints. — [Mobile Free to Play](https://mobilefreetoplay.com/?p=735) (undated, likely older)
- Vendor claims I could not verify:
  - Online multiplayer games show "2x higher long-term retention" than single-player games, attributed to CrazyGames developer docs.
  - Async PvP shows "x2.5 ARPU" vs synchronous.
  — [Edgegap blog](https://edgegap.com/blog/from-puzzles-to-pvp-why-legacy-media-s-gaming-play-will-likely-lead-to-hybrid-casual) and search summary. Treat both as marketing claims.

### Inferences
- The proven casual 1v1 formats are:
  - turn-based or async board games (Ludo, chess)
  - short real-time physics or skill duels (pool)
  - simultaneous "race the same puzzle" duels (Match Masters, Emoji Blitz face-off)
  - io arenas
- All of them reuse the single-player mechanic, so one game loop serves both modes. This fits the brief.
- The Squad Busters shutdown and the declines of Stumble Guys and Brawl Stars suggest that PvP needs a large, steady player pool. Inference: a small browser team should design for async play or "vs ghost/bot fallback" so matchmaking never stalls.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024–2026 hard data for Words With Friends, Trivia Crack, UNO! or 1v1.LOL.
- I found no published head-to-head retention comparison of a 1v1 mode vs a solo mode within the same game from a non-vendor source.
- Deconstructor of Fun coverage did not surface in my searches.

## 3. Trends: hybrid-casual, short sessions, skill-based competitive casual, async play, daily challenges, social sharing, ad monetization

### Takeaway
Hybrid-casual is the growth segment, and rewarded ads are rising fast. Daily-puzzle formats (NYT) are huge but saw Wordle decline in 2025. Skill-based cash competition (Skillz) is a small niche with about 141K paying MAU.

### Cited Findings
- Hybrid-casual IAP grew about 20% in 2025, and top hybrid-casual titles now beat top casual titles on D7 retention. — [Sensor Tower SoG 2026 summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); [Global Games Forum](https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/state-of-gaming-heres-what-the-mobile-data-actually-says)
- Live-ops features with the highest success rate at producing revenue spikes: event menus (83.4%), then expeditions, then battle passes. — [Sensor Tower SoG 2026 summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- NYT Games, 2025:
  - about 11.2B puzzle plays in total
  - Wordle about 4.2B, down from about 5.3B in 2024
  - Connections about 1.6B
  - Mini, Connections and Strands each passed 1B plays
  - The NYT had 12.33M subscribers by Sept 2025.
  — [NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/entertainment/entertainment-news/hardest-wordle-connections-games-2025/6436464/); [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/new-york-times-games-wordle-crossplay-design-jonathan-knight/); [AOL/AP](https://www.aol.com/articles/just-york-times-first-2-140209355.html)
- Skillz, FY2025:
  - revenue $104.5M (about +13%)
  - net loss $70.4M
  - 141K paying MAU in Q4 2025 (−9% QoQ after a large developer left; +28% YoY)
  - ARPPU $61.7
  - a $75M developer accelerator for skill-based games
  — [Business Wire, 31 Mar 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260331578872/en/); [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/19/skillz-sklz-q4-2025-earnings-transcript/)
- Ads: rewarded ads +53.9% YoY to 17.7% of impressions, and playable creatives at 13.3%. — [Sensor Tower SoG 2026 summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Block Blast's brand search score is 8.6, ahead of Candy Crush's 7.4, which indicates strong organic or social pull. — same source
- 28% of casual installs from non-gaming publishers come from utility apps. — [Liftoff/Newswire](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/28-of-casual-game-installs-from-non-gaming-publishers-come-from-utility-apps-liftoff-s-casual-gaming-apps-report-reveals-896981558.html)

### Inferences
- A "daily challenge + shareable result + optional duel" design combines the NYT pattern with the Ludo and pool pattern. Even NYT moved toward 2-player play in 2026.
- Skillz-style real-money competition is a small market (141K payers) and carries regulatory risk. It is not a model to copy for a browser team.

### Gaps
- I found no quantified data on how much social sharing (the Wordle effect) contributes to acquisition.
- I found no 2024–2026 data on async vs sync multiplayer adoption across the market.

## 4. Retention and session-length benchmarks

### Takeaway
2025 mobile medians from GameAnalytics are very low: D1 about 22%, D7 just under 4%, D30 about 0.7–0.8%. Top-10% games reach about 40% D1 and 11–12% D7. I found no current genre-level casual split; the genre figures available are old.

### Cited Findings
**GameAnalytics 2026 report (2025 data, 16,262 mobile games)** — [GameDevReports summary, Jun 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-mobile-and-pc-game); [search summary](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-27-2026-mobile-pc-benchmarks_compressed.pdf)

| Metric | Median | Top 25% | Top 10% | Top 1% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 retention | about 22% (early 2025) | just over 30% | about 40% | 64–68% |
| D7 retention | just under 4% | 6–7% | 11–12% | above 25% (up to about 28%) |
| D30 retention | 0.69–0.79% | 1.6–1.8% | n/a | 13–15% |
| Session length | n/a | about 5.2 min | about 8 min | about 22 min |
| Sessions per day | n/a | 5.3–5.7 | about 9.6 | about 14 |
| Daily playtime | n/a | 22–24 min | 40–42 min | 94+ min |

**Other benchmarks**
- Q1 2024 medians: D1 22.91%, D7 4.2%, D28 0.85%. — [GameAnalytics Q1'24 summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-benchmarks-in-mobile)
- Adjust: average D1 across all gaming apps in 2025 was 27%. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mobile-accounts-for-55-of-total-games-revenue-in-2025-as-studios-shift-towards-retention)
- Old genre data (2018–19 GameAnalytics): D1 arcade 42% and casual 40% (top performers); D7 about 15%. Treat as outdated. — [GameAnalytics hypercasual snapshot](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-hypercasual-games-metrics)
- D7 retention of the top 25 casual games has declined since early 2022. Tasty Travels is cited at about 22% D7 as an exception. — [Sensor Tower SoG 2026 summary](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)

### Inferences
- A browser casual game hitting about 30% D1 and 6% D7 would be in the top quartile. Mobile benchmarks are not directly comparable to browser portals, though.
- Top-quartile sessions are about 5 minutes, which matches a 1–3-minute duel format with several sessions per day.

### Gaps
- I found no 2024–2026 D1/D7/D30 split by genre (puzzle, word, board, io) from a public source. GameAnalytics GameIntel and Liftoff's report tables are gated.
- I found no browser or web-game-specific retention benchmarks (CrazyGames, Poki) in this pass.
